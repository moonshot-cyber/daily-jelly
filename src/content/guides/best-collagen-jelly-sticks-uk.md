Our current top pick overall is BB LAB Pomegranate Collagen S — the closest thing to the original Olive Young-famous jelly stick, with clearly labelled collagen and pomegranate amounts. But "best" depends on what you actually need: a UK-based brand, a vegan option, the highest dose, or simply Amazon's own checkout. Here are our current picks across six real, currently-available UK-reachable sticks, each checked against the retailer's own listing.

## Best overall: BB LAB Pomegranate Collagen S

£18–24 for 14 sticks. The Olive Young-famous format — the closest thing to "the original" Korean jelly stick, with fish collagen and pomegranate concentrate amounts clearly labelled. No UK high-street stockist, so it's an international checkout either way, via YesStyle or Stylevana.

## Best for UK retailer choice: FOODOLOGY Collagen Centella Jelly

£14–22 for 10 sticks. Stocked across several Korean-beauty resellers, so easier to find in stock than most, and pairs the collagen with Centella Asiatica, a well-known K-beauty skin-soothing botanical.

## Best high-dose option: Cloo9 Collagen Jelly

£64 for 28 sticks. The highest labelled dose on this list — 5,000mg of marine collagen per stick — sold direct in GBP with UK delivery, no international checkout. Also the most expensive per stick, though the cheapest per 1,000mg.

## Best vegan: YIXO Vegan Collagen Jelly

£26 for a 21-jelly pack (eaten 3 a day, not 1). UK-based and sold direct, for the simplest delivery of any option here. A plant-based collagen-support blend, not real collagen — there's no plant source of animal collagen.

## Best from a brand you already trust: NOW Foods Collagen Jelly Beauty Complex

£16.03 for 10 sticks. NOW Foods is an established supplement brand most UK shoppers already recognise, using VERISOL — the specific, patented bovine peptide behind the best-studied trial data we cover in our [evidence roundup](/guides/do-collagen-jelly-sticks-actually-work).

## Best if you're already on Amazon: HAMCHOROK Rg+ Pomegranate Collagen Jelly Stick

£38.99 for 30 sticks. The most-reviewed jelly stick we track — 727 ratings at 4.4 stars — dispatched by Amazon for Prime-speed delivery and Amazon's own returns process.
