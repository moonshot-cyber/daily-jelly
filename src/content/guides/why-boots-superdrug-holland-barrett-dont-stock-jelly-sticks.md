If you've searched for somewhere to buy a collagen jelly stick "near me," there's a good chance Google pointed you toward Boots, Superdrug, or Holland & Barrett — and there's an equally good chance you didn't find one when you got there. That's not you missing it on the shelf. It's genuinely not stocked.

## We checked

Searching Holland & Barrett's own site search for "jelly stick" returns zero matches — it suggests you check your spelling or try a less specific term, which is Holland & Barrett—speak for "we don't have that." Boots and Superdrug's own catalogues are, as far as we can find, entirely gummies, capsules, and powders in their collagen range — no jelly-stick format SKU that we could locate.

## Why the gap exists

It's not that these retailers don't sell collagen — they sell plenty, just not in this format. A few structural reasons:

**Retail categorisation.** UK supplement retail treats collagen as a health-aisle product: gummies next to vitamins, powders next to protein. The Korean approach — jelly sticks merchandised in the beauty aisle, next to skincare — doesn't map onto how UK stores are laid out, so there's no obvious shelf for a buyer to place the format even if they wanted to stock it.

**No UK distribution deal yet.** Every jelly-stick brand we cover — BB LAB, FOODOLOGY, Cloo9, YIXO, NOW Foods, HAMCHOROK — sells either direct, through Amazon UK, or through specialist K-beauty resellers. None currently has a UK high-street wholesale/distribution arrangement with Boots, Superdrug, or Holland & Barrett. That's a business decision on the brands' part as much as a stocking decision on the retailers' part.

**It's still a new format here.** Buyers, and therefore buyers-for-retailers, need to see demand before committing shelf space. That's a genuine chicken-and-egg problem for a format this early in the UK — which is exactly why we started this site.

## Where to actually buy one

Since the high street doesn't have them, every real option is online. Our [full buying guide](/where-to-buy) compares six currently-available products — YesStyle, Cloo9's own site, YIXO's own UK site, Welzo, and Amazon UK all carry genuine jelly-stick SKUs with real UK delivery paths, unlike the three retailers above.

If a UK high-street chain does start stocking the format, we'll update this page — that's a real gap in provision right now, not a permanent one.
