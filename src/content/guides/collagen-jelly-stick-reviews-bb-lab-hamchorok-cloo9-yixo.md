Our [where-to-buy guide](/where-to-buy) is built for comparing specs and buying. This is the more opinionated version — six real products, what we actually think of each, and who should skip them.

## BB LAB Pomegranate Collagen S — the one to start with

If you want to understand what the fuss is about, this is the stick to try first. It's the closest thing to "the original" — an Olive Young staple, heavy on real pomegranate concentrate (18,600mg per stick, by far the most fruit-forward of the six), with a clearly labelled 1,000mg marine collagen dose. Nothing about it feels like a compromise version of a Korean product for export.

**Skip it if:** you want the format but object to ordering internationally — there's no UK stockist, so YesStyle or Stylevana checkout is unavoidable.

## FOODOLOGY Collagen Centella Jelly — the easiest to actually find

FOODOLOGY shows up across more Korean-beauty resellers than any other brand here, which counts for something when SKUs from smaller brands go in and out of stock. The Centella Asiatica angle is a nice point of difference — it's a genuinely well-regarded skin-soothing ingredient in Korean skincare, even if we can't verify an exact collagen mg figure from the listing.

**Skip it if:** you specifically want a fully-dosed, fully-transparent label — this is the one product on our list where we couldn't confirm the collagen amount.

## Cloo9 Collagen Jelly — highest dose, highest price

At 5,000mg of marine collagen per stick, Cloo9 has by far the largest dose here — genuinely double the next-highest. It's also a UK-facing DTC brand, so checkout and delivery are straightforward in a way the Korean imports aren't. The trade-off is real: £64 for 28 sticks works out to roughly £2.29 each, the most expensive per-stick price on this list.

**Skip it if:** budget matters more than dose — you can get three sticks from cheaper brands for the price of one Cloo9.

## YIXO Vegan Collagen Jelly — the only genuinely vegan option

If you want the format without an animal-derived ingredient, YIXO is currently the only choice on this list — and it's UK-based, so delivery is fast. Worth reading the label carefully though: this is a plant-based collagen-*support* blend, not collagen itself (there's no vegan source of actual collagen), and the serving size is unusual — three jellies a day for seven days, not one a day like the others. Do the maths on real daily cost before assuming the £26 pack price is comparable.

**Skip it if:** you specifically want marine or bovine collagen and vegan isn't a requirement — you'll get a higher dose and lower daily cost elsewhere.

## NOW Foods Collagen Jelly Beauty Complex — the trust-your-retailer pick

NOW Foods is a recognisable, established supplement brand, sold through Welzo rather than a Korean importer or marketplace reseller — if you'd rather buy from a name you already know than take a chance on an unfamiliar Korean brand, this is the pick. It's also the one product on this list backed by a specific, well-designed clinical study on its exact peptide (VERISOL) — see our [evidence roundup](/guides/do-collagen-jelly-sticks-actually-work) for the actual numbers.

**Skip it if:** you're specifically chasing the Korean "inner beauty" format and branding — this reads as a conventional Western supplement in jelly form, not a K-beauty snack.

## HAMCHOROK Rg+ Pomegranate Collagen Jelly Stick — most-reviewed by far

727 ratings at 4.4 stars on Amazon UK makes this the single most-reviewed product on our list, and it's genuinely useful to have that much real buyer feedback behind a jelly stick rather than the handful of reviews most of these brands have. Dispatched by Amazon means Prime-speed delivery too. The Amazon listing itself leans hard on unproven claims (anti-ageing, immunity) in its title and bullets — ignore those and judge it on the ingredients (pomegranate concentrate, fermented black ginseng, low-molecular fish collagen), which are genuine.

**Skip it if:** you don't want to wade through overclaimed marketing copy to get to the actual ingredient list — though that's true of most supplement listings on Amazon, not unique to this one.

## If you only try one

Start with **BB LAB** if you want the authentic Korean original, or **HAMCHOROK** if you'd rather buy somewhere you already have an account and trust the review volume. Full pricing, delivery paths, and a side-by-side compare table are in our [where-to-buy guide](/where-to-buy).
