If you've seen American TikTok or Instagram posts about a huge box of collagen jelly sticks from Costco, that's real — and no, Costco UK doesn't have it. Here's what's actually going on, and the closest you can get here.

## What's on Costco.com

[EVERYDAZE](/guides/vegan-and-plant-based-collagen-jelly-sticks) — the same vegan jelly-stick brand already in our guide — sells a 42-count value pack exclusively through Costco's US website, in both Peach and Pomegranate flavours, at **$33.99**. That works out to roughly **$0.81 per stick** — meaningfully cheaper than anything in our UK comparison, where per-stick prices generally run £1.24–2.29.

## Why it's US-only

This is a straightforward retail distribution gap, not a product difference. Costco's US warehouse membership model is built around exactly this kind of bulk value pack, and EVERYDAZE has a US distribution deal that gets it onto Costco.com. Costco UK's online and warehouse ranges are decided separately, and as of publishing there's no equivalent listing on Costco UK's site — this format hasn't reached UK bulk retail in any form yet, matching everything else we've found about [why the UK high street doesn't stock jelly sticks](/guides/why-boots-superdrug-holland-barrett-dont-stock-jelly-sticks).

## The closest UK equivalent

You can still buy EVERYDAZE itself in the UK — just not at Costco bulk pricing. YesStyle sells the same product in 10-stick packs for roughly £11.94–14.74 (about £1.19–1.47 a stick, depending on current promotions) — a real UK delivery path, just without the bulk discount that a 42-count warehouse pack gets you in the US.

If bulk value specifically matters more to you than brand, [Cloo9's 28-stick pack](/where-to-buy) and [MIZON's 2×14-stick bundle](/guides/vegan-and-plant-based-collagen-jelly-sticks) are the largest single UK-bought packs in our guides — still smaller than Costco's 42-count, but closer than a standard 10-stick box.

## Should you import from US Costco?

We wouldn't recommend it. Beyond the shipping cost likely erasing the per-stick saving, importing a food supplement from the US for personal use can run into customs and food-import rules that aren't worth the hassle for a snack-format product you can already buy properly through a UK-facing retailer. The saving is real on paper — it doesn't survive international shipping in practice.
